How scores are selected

Paper values take precedence whenever the corresponding dataset, metric and evaluation setting are reported. Saved runs and differences remain in Details. Completed runs without a paper counterpart have their own setting. Dataset rankings are grouped by protocol and metric. The main overall ranking uses an equal-weight mean of each method’s available datasets, with one score per dataset. All methods receive ranks. Different dataset coverage and evaluation settings are marked with * and explained at the bottom of the page.

Paper imports retain their source and review notes; the label does not claim an independent re-evaluation. Incomplete runs do not receive scores.
