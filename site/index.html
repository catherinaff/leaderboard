How scores are selected

Paper values take precedence whenever the corresponding dataset, metric and evaluation setting are reported. Saved runs and differences remain in Details. Completed runs without a paper counterpart have their own setting. Dataset rankings are grouped by protocol and metric. Overall preserves original paper averages when the full target set and setting match. Other means use equal dataset weight across a fixed, visible set; partial coverage remains unranked.

Paper imports retain their source and review notes; the label does not claim an independent re-evaluation. Incomplete runs do not receive scores.
