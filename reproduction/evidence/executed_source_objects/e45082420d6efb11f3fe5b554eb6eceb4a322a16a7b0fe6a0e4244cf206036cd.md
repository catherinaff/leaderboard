## Data and protocol

`prepare.py` uses the original archives and writes immutable native windows,
recording/start provenance, SHA-256 manifests and all115 subject/seed splits.
Windows do not cross recordings. Labels use a majority of non-null observations.
Recording-local forward fill leaves leading NaNs for a training-only median.
Each run fits its scaler on training windows, saves validation-selected weights,
and emits held-out predictions. It never selects a model on test performance.

UCI uses its7352/2947 official windows and stratified10% validation within train.
WISDM uses subjects1–28/29–30/31–36. LOSO validation is the next subject in the
fixed sorted subject list. HHAR is aligned/resampled to50Hz, without joining time
gaps or devices. WISDM's corrupted/duplicate timestamps are not interpolated;
native nominal20Hz order is retained with subject/large-time-gap boundaries.
The rejected timestamp-interpolation cache is preserved separately and unused.

Method-required sensor adaptations are recorded. IF-ConvTransformer uses physical
gravity/gyro/body-acc streams and the author's phone/wearable models. UniMTS uses
its strict-loaded published IMU branch, physical joint mapping, Fourier resampling
and author wrap padding. Unsupported magnetic/extra shoe channels are excluded
explicitly. LIMU-BERT preserves all native channels, pretrains on training windows,
and uses the author GRU on20-step embedding chunks. It aggregates probabilities
back to whole benchmark windows. No old first-six/first-nine truncation is reused.

DeepConvLSTM preserves peephole connections and Lasagne RMSprop's epsilon inside
the square root. DeepSense preserves TF1 reset-before GRU math, source FFT order,
fusion padding and the concatenated-kernel initialization distribution.
RobustHAR's unreported dimensions and ambiguous equations are documented in
`ROBUSTHAR_AUDIT.md`; they are not asserted to be the authors' exact settings.

  publication, model-fingerprint gating and exclusion of superseded scores.
